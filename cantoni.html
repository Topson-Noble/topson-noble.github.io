Cantoni

A simple, highscoring game built with Unreal Engine, utilizing point-and-click mechanics and a leaderboard system.

Project Overview

Cantoni is a lighthearted, casual point-and-click game built around a dynamic leaderboard system. The game was designed as both a fun interactive experience and a personal technical challenge to implement player data persistence and scoring mechanics within a complete gameplay loop.

The title “Cantoni” takes its name from the original soundtrack composed for the game, a lively one-minute track that sets the rhythm and tone for this cheerful, slightly chaotic world where happiness is constantly under attack.

Gameplay Concept

Players enter their name, which the game remembers for later leaderboard ranking. During gameplay, players click on mischievous bubbles before they reach the three joyful friends at the center of the screen.

Each bubble popped earns points, while missed ones reduce happiness. Gameplay lasts one minute, synced to the Cantoni soundtrack.

Leaderboard & Progression

Persistent leaderboard shows top 10 scores ("Top Poppers") with real-time ranking. Returning players can compare their new score with previous ones to encourage replayability.

Design & Aesthetics

- Visual Style: Clear motion-focused minimal design.

- Characters: Three expressive looping friends.

- Antagonist: Spiky bubble-shooting creature.

- Audio: Original background track synchronized with gameplay.

- UI: Clean interface for player input, score tracking, and leaderboard display.

Development Details

- Engine: Unreal Engine 5.5.4

- Role: Solo developer – design, programming, audio, visuals.

- Platforms: PC (Windows)

Technical & Creative Highlights

- Custom leaderboard system with persistent score saving.

- Data-driven player naming and scoring system.

- Enemy AI spawning projectiles dynamically.

- Timed gameplay synchronized with soundtrack.

Reflection

Cantoni combines creative design and technical problem-solving. This project refined skills in UI scripting, event logic, and timing-based gameplay within a self-contained development cycle.
