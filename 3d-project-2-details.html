3D Animation Demo Reel

This reel is a compilation of several animation exercises and personal projects created to demonstrate my versatility and skill as a 3D Animator and CG Artist.

Project Overview

This reel is a compilation of several animation exercises and personal projects created to demonstrate my versatility and skill as a 3D Character Animator. It brings together a mix of acting, body mechanics, and pantomime performances, each designed to showcase specific animation principles such as weight, timing, emotion, anticipation, and rhythm.

Technical Details

All animations were created in Autodesk Maya, with final rendering done using VRay. Character rigs used in this reel are free online rigs. The goal of the project was to highlight storytelling through motion, weight distribution, and expressive timing across varied contexts.

Role & Focus

- Sole Animator on all shots.

- Responsible for blocking, polishing, and camera composition.

- Focused on performance, timing, and expressive body language.

- Used different lighting setups and render styles to suit each mood.

Objective

This demo reel serves as a visual summary of my growth and range as an animator, bringing together both technical precision and emotional storytelling.
