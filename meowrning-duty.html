Project Overview

Meowrning Duty is a short, lighthearted game developed for the 2025 Epic MegaJam, centered around the theme "Here we go again." The project explores the idea of repetition and daily routine through a fun, quirky lens, putting the player in control of a cat-shaped alarm clock whose eternal mission is to wake its sleepy human every single morning.

Gameplay Concept

Built around a fast-paced button-mashing mechanic, the player must repeatedly tap the 'E' key to charge a wake-up meter before the timer runs out. Succeed, and the alarm rings out with a triumphant meow, the human wakes up, and a new day begins. Fail, and the human keeps snoozing, leaving the poor cat alarm to endure another chorus of snoring.

The gameplay loop is intentionally simple, repetitive, and comedic, capturing the essence of the jam's theme: doing the same thing over and over again. Each new day offers slight variations in visuals, timing, and tone, reinforcing the monotony and humor of the cat's endless duty.

Narrative & Tone

The game's tone is playful yet subtly existential. Every morning feels familiar, yet slightly different. The cat alarm grows increasingly desperate and resigned, perfectly embodying the "Here we go again" theme.

Players encounter varying intro and ending lines, adding personality and charm to the otherwise minimalist loop, and making each attempt feel fresh.

Design & Aesthetics

- Visual Style: Clean, minimalist and stylized aesthetic.

- Character Design: The main character, a cat-shaped alarm clock, reinforces the comedic identity of the game. Each button press emits a "meow," culminating in a classic alarm ring when successful.

- UI/UX: Built around clarity and timing precision, ensuring that players immediately understand the objective and rhythm.

Development Details

- Engine: Unreal Engine 5.5.4

- Timeframe: Designed and developed within the limited window of the Epic MegaJam (approx. 12 hours of active work).

- Role: Solo developer, responsible for game design, programming, UI, animation timing, audio design, and overall polish.

- Platforms: PC (Windows), with planned builds for Android and macOS.

Technical & Creative Highlights

- Implemented a reactive button-mash system that responds to speed and accuracy.

- Designed multiple outcome states (success/fail) with randomized text feedback for replay variety.

- Integrated custom sound design, including unique "meow" cues that escalate into alarm rings.

- Focused on strong theme integration, repetition, frustration, and humor, all wrapped in a simple, replayable experience.

Reflection

Meowrning Duty demonstrates my ability to ideate, prototype, and polish a complete experience within strict time constraints, while maintaining creativity, consistency, and humor. It's a small game that embraces limitation, turning simplicity into personality.
